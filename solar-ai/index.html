The analysis

Three questions, answered from your own data.

  1. Plant model

    How much should the plant have made?

    A physical model calibrated on your healthiest blocks, fed with the irradiance, temperature and rear-side light each block actually saw.

  2. Losses

    Where did the missing energy go?

    A waterfall from expected to exported energy: outages, curtailment, inverters, DC buses, trackers, clipping and the unexplained rest.

  3. Faults & events

    What happened, when, and to which equipment?

    Every episode found automatically, with start, duration, energy lost and its value, down to a single DC bus or tracker.

Why market value matters

Not every lost megawatt-hour costs the same.

In the demo period, 43 % of the lost energy fell in intervals when the market paid nothing for it. Pricing every MWh at a flat tariff would have overstated the damage more than four times, and sent the maintenance team after the wrong problem.

Method

From your SCADA export to a ranked list of actions.

No hardware and no software to install. We work from the exports your SCADA already produces.

  1. You share the data

    SCADA exports, the plant layout and, if you have it, the design energy simulation. A secure folder is enough.

  2. We clean and check it

    Bad and frozen readings out, weather stations cross-checked against each other, gaps flagged rather than filled.

  3. We model and price

    The plant modelled block by block, every loss located and explained, and each one valued at market prices or your tariff.

  4. We walk you through it

    The report and dashboard delivered, and a review session with our analyst to agree the first actions.

Questions

Before you send us any data.

Do we need to install anything?

No. We work from the exports your SCADA system already produces. Nothing connects to your plant's network.

How much data do you need?

One month of five-minute data is enough for a first diagnostic. A full year lets us separate seasonal effects and calibrate the long-term forecast with more confidence.

What happens to our data?

It is used only for your analysis and is never published. Any figure we show outside your company, like the demo on this page, comes from a fictitious plant with data we generated.
